Snippets Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. This is a starting draft. Have it reviewed before publishing.

Snippets is a private weekly scrapbook you share with a few friends. We collect only what we need for that.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, or the identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google. Also the display name, username and optional profile photo you choose.
  • Your moments: the photos and short videos you choose to add, with the titles, reflections, reactions and comments you write. We store compressed copies on our servers so your archive survives if you lose your phone. We don't access the rest of your photo library.
  • Your timezone, so "Sunday" means your Sunday.
  • Device push token, if you allow notifications.
  • A few usage events, such as opening the app, sharing your week, viewing friends' weeks or sending an invite. These are stored in our own database, linked to your account, and contain no titles, reflections or photos. We use them only to understand whether Snippets is working, for example how many people share each week.

We don't collect location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, we don't use third-party analytics or ad SDKs, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your content

Your moments are visible only to people you've mutually added as friends. A friend sees a week of yours only after they have shared that same week. There are no public profiles, public posts or search engines. Other signed-in users can find you only by your exact username prefix. They see your display name, username and profile photo, and nothing else.

How we use it

Only to run Snippets: storing your archive, showing it to your friends, and sending the few notifications you've enabled. These are the Sunday reminder, friend nudges, a notice when friends share, and replies to your moments. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Service providers

We host data with Supabase (database, file storage, authentication) and send notifications through Expo's push service, together with Apple and Google push services. These providers process data only on our behalf.

Deleting your data

You can delete moments, comments and friends at any time. Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account, every photo and video, and all related data from our servers. Backups age out within 30 days. You can also email [SUPPORT EMAIL] to request deletion.

Children

Snippets isn't intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
